Play any video games?

I'm really into rhythm games! The ones I play the most are Maimai DX and Cytus II. Maimai is that one arcade game that looks like a giant washing machine, and Cytus II is like osu!, but on mobile instead of on a computer. I also occasionally play Maplestory.

Hobbies?

I like to draw, play piano, go on long walks, and do personal projects!

For drawing, my favorite mediums are oil paints on canvas and charcoals on toned paper, but those are a bit messy and hard to do as a college student :( so the medium I end up using most often is digital art where I use Procreate. I might make an art page on my website later.

My favorite composers to play on the piano are Rachmaninoff and Chopin, since I love how melodramatic and moody their pieces are. Unfortunately, Rachmaninoff’s piano writing is notoriously unfriendly to small hands, but I still (somehow) make it work.

I love walking in the night time, since the lack of people around is calm and peaceful. Also it tends to be much cooler out at night, meaning I can walk much further without tiring or getting sweaty.
